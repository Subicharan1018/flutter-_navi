NaviVibe 2.0.0 - everything new since build 184

Playback
- Fast start: songs begin from a small 64 kbps copy while the full one downloads, then swap in at the same position. Turn it off in Settings > Playback.
- Skips settle: songs you skip past are never opened, and preloading waits for the current song.
- The next songs preload after 25% or 20 seconds, swap in with a short ramp, and the player shows the live quality badge.
- Network drops no longer stop, reload or skip the song that is playing; the offline notice shows once per outage and clears itself after a stable reconnect.
- A queue restored while offline stages songs that are not on the device and loads them when the network returns or when you press Play.
- A fix keeps the app-wide audio handler alive when the player rebuilds.

Design
- Artist page: a plain "With You" story (plays, listening time, first and latest play with how long you have known them, most played) and an album list by year with your play counts. No charts.
- Stats: each card has its own form. A frosted hero over your top artist's picture with your listener type, a ticket for your streak, an embossed dial for listening hours, a typographic genre poster and a labelled Smart Shuffle model meter.
- Replay, Smart Shuffle, Favorites, Highlights and Start Listening have their own artwork drawn from your own covers instead of the 2x2 grid (playlists keep it).
- "This device is the host" is one quiet line of text instead of a pill.
- Motion is purposeful and small, plays once or only while a card is on screen, and stays still with Reduced motion.

Peak moments
- Save a moment from Now Playing, play your highlight reel, and see your best listening moments.

Devices and sync
- Taps and Play go to the device that is actually playing.
- Send new music to any of your devices.
- Sync keeps retrying after a failed re-auth, and hung connections time out.

Replay
- History titles with film, language or "feat." tags (for example "Monica (From Coolie) (Tamil)") now match your library songs, without merging distinct versions such as Unplugged, Remix or Live.

Settings
- Settings are grouped into nine priority-ordered folders (Playback first), each opening its own page, on phone and TV; Windows keeps its sidebar with the same folders.
- Microduck is off until you turn it on.

Windows and desktop
- NaviVibe can register as a music player: .mp3 .flac .m4a .ogg .opus .wav appear under Default apps, open files from Explorer or the command line, and a second launch hands the file to the running app. A Linux .desktop entry is included.

Speed, battery and size
- Own cover cache (2000 files, 90 days) with four server cover sizes.
- Indexed song sorting columns, fewer library rebuilds, throttled download progress, a cheaper artwork animation (30 fps), invite polls only while the app is in the foreground, a display-rate glass budget, low-RAM defaults, unbundled ML Kit and a subset Inter font.
- Web: precompressed Brotli/gzip and cross-origin isolation headers.

Fixes
- Library sync ends only on an empty page; large playlist song lists are sent as a request body.
- Social picker, online search timeouts, history import and other screens no longer act after they are closed.
- Personal LAN addresses were removed from the app's source.

Under the hood
- Dead and duplicate code removed, one place for external hosts, named theme and breakpoint tokens, and platform checks routed through one helper.
